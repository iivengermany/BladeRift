          Bladerift

          A twin-blade arena. Collect shards to grow, then cut down rivals and take what they drop. 30 bladers share the rift with you. To play with a friend, one of you presses Host and shares the room code, the other types it in and presses Join.

          Steer
          Pointer, finger or WASD.
          Dash
          Space or the Dash button. Dashing hits harder.
          Blades
          Click, press E or tap Detach to drop them anywhere. Hold to pull them to the nearest border. Walk over them to reattach.
          Core
          The walled Core in the middle has a gate on its left and one on its right. Entering detaches your blades, and what you collect there waits in your backpack (1,000 max) until you reattach. Its hole cycles blue, red, purple.
          Corners
          Red corners burn 20% of your shards. With blades detached they kill. Take the red core out of the Core and reattach for 2 minutes of overdrive.
